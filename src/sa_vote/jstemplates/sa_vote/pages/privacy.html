{{#_}}Privacy Policy{{/_}}

{{#_}}We designed the Ideas in Action voting process to protect your privacy at every step. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you take part in Boston's participatory budgeting vote.{{/_}}

{{#_}}Ballot Choices{{/_}}

{{#_}}Your ballot choices are completely anonymous. We never connect your votes to your phone number, name, or identity.{{/_}}

{{#_}}We use your votes only to determine which ballot proposals to fund in this cycle.{{/_}}

{{#_}}Demographic Survey{{/_}}

{{#_}}After you vote, you can complete a short demographic survey. This survey is completely optional.{{/_}}

{{#_}}Your survey responses are anonymous and stored separately from your ballot. We use this data only to understand how people connect with the Ideas in Action process, and to make sure all Bostonians are represented. You can skip any questions you do not want to answer.{{/_}}

{{#_}}Phone Numbers and Text Messages{{/_}}

{{#_}}When you register to vote, we ask for your mobile phone number. We use phone numbers only to verify voter uniqueness and make sure each voter casts only one ballot.{{/_}}

  • {{#_}}One-time verification code: We send you a single verification code by text message so you can vote. We will not send any further text message communication.{{/_}}
  • {{#_}}No plain text storage: We will never store your phone number in plain text. We only store an encrypted hashed version of your phone number to uniquely identify voters.{{/_}}
  • {{#_}}No third-party marketing: We will not share, sell, rent, or trade your phone number or mobile messaging data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.{{/_}}

{{#_}}Email Addresses{{/_}}

{{#_}}If you share your email address with us, we only share it with Boston's Office of Participatory Budgeting for mailing list purposes.{{/_}}

{{#_}}We will never link your email address to your ballot choices or your survey responses.{{/_}}

{{#_}}Questions and Contact{{/_}}

{{#_}}If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact Boston's Office of Participatory Budgeting by email at pb@boston.gov.{{/_}}
